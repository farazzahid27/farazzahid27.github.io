About

I'm a process engineer with 8+ years of experience delivering FEED, detailed design, commissioning, start-up and EPCM projects across battery chemicals, mining, hydrometallurgy and novel industrial processing.

Currently, I'm a Senior Process Design Engineer at Sweco Finland, where I lead process design and take engineering management roles on projects for international clients across Europe. I work from early feasibility studies through to cold and hot commissioning, with a focus on process safety, optimisation and getting multidisciplinary teams to deliver together.

I'm also passionate about digitalisation. At Sweco I build AI-enabled engineering tools and help colleagues put them into everyday use, automating repetitive work so engineers can spend more time on engineering.
