About

About me

I am a Senior Process Design Engineer with more than eight years of experience delivering industrial projects from concept development and feasibility studies through detailed design, commissioning and start-up. My experience spans battery chemicals, mining, hydrometallurgy, specialty chemicals, and emerging industrial technologies, with responsibilities covering process design, engineering management, multidisciplinary coordination, process safety, commissioning, and operational readiness.

At Sweco Finland Oy, I serve as a Process Lead, Engineering Manager, and Deputy Engineering Manager on international EPCM projects, leading engineering teams and coordinating stakeholders to deliver complex industrial projects for clients across Europe. Throughout my career, I have been passionate about turning technical concepts into practical, reliable, and efficient industrial solutions while maintaining a strong focus on safety, quality, and project execution.

Alongside traditional process engineering, I actively work at the intersection of engineering and technology. I develop engineering tools, automate repetitive workflows, and explore how digital engineering, automation, and Artificial Intelligence can improve productivity, knowledge sharing, and decision-making within engineering organisations. I also support colleagues and project teams in adopting practical AI solutions that can be integrated into everyday engineering work.

Beyond tool development, I regularly deliver AI awareness sessions, demonstrations, and training workshops for engineers and project teams, helping them understand how emerging technologies can be applied safely, effectively, and responsibly. My goal is to bridge the gap between traditional engineering expertise and modern digital tools, enabling engineers to spend less time on routine tasks and more time solving complex technical challenges.

I believe the future of engineering lies in combining deep domain expertise with intelligent digital solutions, creating organisations that are not only technically strong but also more efficient, innovative, and adaptable to the challenges of tomorrow.
